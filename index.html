About Me

Product Builder. Full Stack Engineer. Founder.

I'm a Full Stack Developer with 6 years of production experience across web platforms, mobile applications, and API-driven systems. I've built everything from academic management platforms and ERP tools to consumer-facing marketplaces — owning delivery from database design through deployment.

I currently work at Ramel Techno Solutions in Chennai, building scalable backend systems with Laravel microservices, REST APIs. Prior to this, I spent 4 years at Arkmedis Edutaint, building and scaling production-grade platforms used in real academic and operational workflows. During this time, I contributed to and led development across multiple domains, including CRM, ERP, and e-commerce systems, along with cross-platform mobile applications using Flutter. I was actively involved in end-to-end development, handling both frontend and backend, designing scalable architectures, developing robust APIs, and integrating third-party services. My work focused on delivering reliable, high-performance solutions that support real-world business processes and user needs.

Beyond employer work, I'm the founder of UTSAVPLANS — an event service marketplace I conceived, designed, and shipped independently. That project sharpened my product thinking and deepened my appreciation for end-to-end ownership.

I think in systems, care about architecture, and take full ownership of delivery. Whether it's backend logic, mobile UX, VPS deployment, or business flow design — I stay in the loop end to end.

Based in Chennai, India. Open to remote roles, freelance contracts, and product collaborations.
